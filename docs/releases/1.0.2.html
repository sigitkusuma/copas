Copas 1.0.2

The clipboard board is now two panes. Everything you have copied runs down the left, newest first and still grouped by day, and the whole of whichever clip you are on is shown beside it. Nothing is truncated any more — it is either in the list or in the pane next to it.

  • Arrows turn ninety degrees with the layout. Up and down move between clips, and Option with them jumps a whole day at a time. Page Up and Page Down move a screenful. Left and right go back to the search field, where they belong.
  • The mouse works too. Click a clip to read it, double-click to paste it.
  • Search marks what it found in the full text on the right, not only in the list — so a match buried deep in a long clip is visible rather than merely counted.

Fixed

  • Settings now opens. It never did: the window macOS was asked for is only ever shown for apps that keep a Dock icon, and the request reported success regardless, so the menu item did nothing at all and said nothing about it. Settings is now a window Copas builds itself, and ⌘W closes it.

Your clips, settings and shortcuts are untouched by this update.
