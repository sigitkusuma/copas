Copas 1.1.0

A major release introducing seamless clipboard history synchronization across all your Macs using private iCloud and CloudKit.

  • CloudKit Synchronization: Sync your clipboard history across your Macs securely. Clips—including rich text, large text, and images—are synchronized automatically across devices signed into the same Apple ID.
  • Private by Design & Opt-In: Sync is completely opt-in and disabled by default. Your data is stored solely in your private CloudKit database with no external servers, accounts, or analytics.
  • Instant Remote Push Updates: Changes made on one Mac appear almost instantaneously on other Macs through CloudKit database subscriptions and background notifications.
  • Smart Conflict Resolution & Deletions: Seamless newest-wins conflict resolution and cross-device deletion tracking keep your history in lockstep without duplicate cards.
  • Dedicated Sync Settings: A new Sync tab in Settings allows you to easily toggle synchronization, view your iCloud connection status, inspect pending uploads, and trigger manual syncs on demand.

Your existing local clips, pinned items, and settings are preserved seamlessly.
