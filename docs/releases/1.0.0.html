Copas 1.0

The first release. A clipboard manager that lives in the menu bar: press ⇧⌘V and everything you have copied is a strip of cards under the menu bar, newest first, grouped by day. Arrow to one, press Return, and it pastes into whatever you were just typing in.

What it does

  • Keeps formatting. Copy something bold and it pastes back bold.
  • Search that reaches the whole clip, not just its first line — and the text inside your screenshots. Type anywhere on the board to start, or narrow it with app:xcode, type:image, has:text.
  • Reads text in images as they arrive, on your Mac, through Vision. A screenshot of a receipt is findable by what the receipt says.
  • ⇧⌘2 captures a region of the screen as text — a receipt, an error dialog, something somebody sent you as a picture.
  • Skips what it should. Anything a password manager marks as concealed is never read, never hashed, and never written to disk. Other apps can be excluded by hand.

Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac. No account, no sync, no analytics. Text recognition runs locally. The only network request Copas makes is to its own update feed.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later. Universal — Apple silicon and Intel. Signed and notarised by Apple.
