Copas 1.0.3

A round of fit-and-finish on the board.

  • Clicking a clip is instant. Selecting one used to wait out the double-click interval before it registered, because a single click and a double click were competing gestures on the same row. A click now selects immediately; a second click still pastes.
  • Clips in the list have room to breathe instead of running edge to edge.
  • The keyboard-shortcut legend along the bottom is legible rather than nearly the same colour as the background behind it.
  • The menu bar icon is Copas's own mark now, not a generic system glyph standing in for it.
  • A new look for the mark itself: flat black ink in place of the blue-violet gradient, carried through the whole app as the one accent colour — the selection bar, search highlights, and the search field's own caret.

Your clips, settings and shortcuts are untouched by this update.
