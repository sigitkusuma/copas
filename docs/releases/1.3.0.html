Copas 1.3.0

Screenshots taken with Capture to Image come out sharper, and the editor can now enhance them.

  • Enhance: A new Enhance button in the editor sharpens text and edges in the copied picture. Marks you draw stay exactly as drawn.
  • Sharper resizing: Shrinking a screenshot now keeps small text crisp and at its full weight, even at 50% or 25%.
  • 200% resize: A new 200% option enlarges captures from non-Retina displays.
  • Untouched means identical: A screenshot you don't change is copied exactly as it was captured.
  • Full colour depth: Captures from HDR and wide-colour displays keep their full colour depth.
