Copas 1.0.6

A major productivity release transforming Copas into a versatile clipboard powerhouse.

  • Pinned Clips (⌘P): Keep your essential snippets permanent. Pinned clips are immune to retention pruning, highlighted with a warm gold pin badge, and organized into a dedicated section at the top of the board. Filter them anytime with is:pinned.
  • Multi-Select & Merge (⌘M): Select multiple clips with ⌘-click or Shift-click, choose your delimiter (paragraphs, newlines, commas, tabs, or spaces), and merge them directly to the pasteboard or export them together.
  • Text Transforms (⌘T): Apply 15 instant formatting transformations to any text clip on the fly — case conversions, whitespace trimming, line sorting, duplicate line removal, line numbering, URL & Base64 encoding/decoding, JSON formatting/minification, quote wrapping, and HTML escaping.
  • Smart Filter Pills: Instant one-tap filter pills beneath search for All, Pinned, Links, Code, Images, and Colors.
  • Native Share Sheet & File Export: Share clips directly via AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Notes, or Reminders, or export them to disk with automatic file extension detection (.txt, .json, .md, .png).
  • Right-Click Context Menu: Right-click any clip in the list to share, export, paste, copy, pin, or delete without opening the detail pane.
  • Clipboard Insights & Statistics: Explore your clipboard habits in the new Stats tab in Settings (⌘,) — total history, text vs. image breakdown, storage footprint, and top source applications with app icons and proportional charts.

Your clips, settings and shortcuts are untouched by this update.
