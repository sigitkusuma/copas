Copas 1.0.5

  • Capture to Image (⇧⌘1) is a new sibling to Capture to Text: drag out a region and keep it as a picture instead of converting it to text — handy for diagrams and mockups, anything better seen than read. It shows up in the menu bar and Settings alongside Capture to Text, and on the Welcome screen for anyone opening Copas for the first time.

Your clips, settings and shortcuts are untouched by this update.
