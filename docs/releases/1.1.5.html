Copas 1.1.5

Fixes iCloud account connection status and enhances the Settings window layout.

  • Settings Layout: Expanded the Settings window width so all six tabs (General, History, Text, Stats, Sync, About) fit directly in the toolbar without needing to click the expand chevron (>>).
  • iCloud Connection: Added missing application-identifier and container-environment entitlements to resolve the "Missing Entitlement: Trying to initialize a container without an application ID" error, enabling the status to transition immediately from "Checking..." to "Connected".
