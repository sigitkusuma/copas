Copas 1.0.9

A productivity and window management release introducing Pin-to-Screen scratchpad mode for sequential multi-pasting, full board movability and dragging, direct action buttons in preview, and bounded clean image rendering.

  • Pin-to-Screen (Scratchpad Mode): Press ⌘⇧P or click the pin icon in the search bar to lock Copas on screen. The board remains open as a persistent floating palette while pasting multiple clips or switching between apps, complete with an accent badge in the status bar.
  • Effortless Sequential Multi-Pasting: Easily paste multiple items into Google Docs, Word, Notes, or Slack without repeatedly reopening the board. New dedicated [Paste] (↩) and [Copy] (⌘↩) buttons in the preview header allow instant one-click pasting.
  • Draggable Board Window: The clipboard manager panel can now be freely dragged and positioned anywhere across displays using the top capsule handle, search bar margins, or bottom status bar.
  • Bounded & Clean Image Display: Image clips now render with responsive bounded dimensions, eliminating oversized overflow and chess/checkerboard background patterns while preserving crisp 1:1 pixel fidelity.
  • Swift 6 Concurrency Updates: Refined actor isolation and concurrency conformance in settings and updater coordinators.

Your existing clips, settings, and database are preserved seamlessly.
