Copas 1.1.3

Enables iCloud Sync via embedded Developer ID provisioning profile.

  • iCloud Sync: The app now includes a provisioning profile that grants the com.apple.developer.icloud-container-identifiers entitlement, allowing CloudKit to initialize without requiring a Mac App Store distribution.
  • Stability: No change to local clipboard history, database, or existing features. The sync engine remains gated behind the Sync toggle in Settings.
